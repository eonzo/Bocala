# My Favorite Movie: *Cars* (2006)

## **Main Characters**
- **Lightning McQueen** – A rookie race car who learns humility and friendship.
- **Mater** – A rusty but loyal tow truck with a big heart.
- **Sally Carrera** – A smart Porsche who cares deeply about Radiator Springs.
- **Doc Hudson** – A retired race car with a legendary past.

## _Plot Summary_
> Lightning McQueen gets stranded in a forgotten town and discovers that winning isn't everything. Through new friendships and challenges, he learns the value of slowing down and appreciating life.

## **Themes**
- 🏁 Growth through adversity  
- 🛠️ Importance of community  
- 💬 True friendship over fame
